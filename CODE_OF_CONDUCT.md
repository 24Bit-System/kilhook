# Code of conduct

Be reasonable to the other people here. Review the change, not the person who
sent it, and assume good intent until there is evidence otherwise.

Not acceptable in issues, pull requests or anywhere else project-related:

- harassment, insults, slurs, or discriminatory remarks
- publishing someone else's private information
- deliberate intimidation or a sustained pattern of disruption

Email info@24bitsystem.com to report something. Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers can remove comments, close threads or block accounts; what happens
depends on what was said and whether it continues.
